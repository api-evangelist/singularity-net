For over 18 years, the AGI Conference has stood as the world’s premier forum for the exploration and advancement of Artificial General Intelligence. From its early days as a bold academic workshop to its current status as a globally recognized gathering of top researchers, philosophers, engineers, and futurists, the AGI Conference continues to define the cutting edge of general-purpose intelligence. With each passing year, the urgency and importance of AGI research grows — and AGI-25 promises to be our most ambitious and globally resonant edition yet.
