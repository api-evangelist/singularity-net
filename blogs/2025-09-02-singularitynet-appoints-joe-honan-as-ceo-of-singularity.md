NEWS, 2nd September , London, UK – SingularityNET , the decentralized AI platform, today announced that Joe Honan has been appointed Chief Executive Officer of Singularity Compute, the ecosystem’s independent, for-profit operating company dedicated to delivering advanced, high-performance AI compute infrastructure and AGI-class solutions. Joe is a seasoned technology executive with decades of success in founding, commercializing, and scaling high-growth technology ventures. Over his career, he has helped build startups into market-leading enterprises with more than $8 billion USD in exit value and has led some of the world’s largest and most complex technology transformation projects.
