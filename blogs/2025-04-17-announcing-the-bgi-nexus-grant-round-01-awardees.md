After much anticipation, we’re proud to announce the results of the first BGI Nexus Grant Round and to celebrate ten groundbreaking projects that our global community has collectively prioritized for funding. The BGI Nexus is a global community dedicated to advancing beneficial AI and AGI through collaborative development, transparent governance, and aligned resourcing. Together, we’re working to ensure that the growing power of AI is directed toward the flourishing of humanity, the biosphere, and future generations.
