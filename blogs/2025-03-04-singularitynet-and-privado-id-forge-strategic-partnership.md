SingularityNET, a founding Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) Alliance Member and a pioneering decentralized AI leader , today announced that it has formalized a strategic technical partnership with Privado ID (formerly Polygon ID) . This collaboration sets the stage for launching the first decentralized AI Agent Trust Registry within the ASI ecosystem. SingularityNET, the ASI Alliance, and Privado ID will jointly develop an innovative AI Agent avatar trust registry.
