The AGI Conference is returning in 2026, and this year it comes to San Francisco. AGI-26 is the 19th edition of the only major conference series devoted wholly and specifically to the creation of AI systems possessing general intelligence at the human level and beyond. Since 2008, it has served as the gathering place for the researchers, engineers, and thinkers who take that goal seriously.
