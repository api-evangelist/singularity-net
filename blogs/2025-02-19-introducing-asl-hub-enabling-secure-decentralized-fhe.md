In decentralized AI (dAI) and blockchain systems, two fundamental challenges hinder their reliability and scalability: secure agent identification and verifiable randomness. AI agents require trust to ensure verifiable identity and trustworthiness in interactions. Meanwhile, randomness is essential for AI processes such as training, governance, and decision-making.
