Empowering innovation by connecting a global network of 35,000 AI/ML experts with SingularityNET’s multi-million dollar OpenCog Hyperon AGI R&D grants and sponsored data science competitions. SingularityNET, a founding member of the Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) Alliance and a pioneer in decentralized AI, today announced a landmark partnership with Bitgrit. This collaboration bridges SingularityNET’s cutting-edge decentralized AI platform with Bitgrit’s active data science community of over 35,000 AI/ML engineers and data scientists.
