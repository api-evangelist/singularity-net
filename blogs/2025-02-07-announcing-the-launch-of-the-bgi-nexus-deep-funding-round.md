The BGI Nexus initiative, in collaboration with Deep Funding, is excited to announce the new BGI Nexus grant cycle. The first grant round will open on February 10th, and be an opportunity to showcase global progress toward AI systems that benefit humanity and the world. Winners will be announced in April, and will attend the BGI Summit and Unconference to present their projects and to network with fellow thought leaders in the space of BGI.
