Open Letter From the COO Dear SingularityNET Community, During 2024, SingularityNET made huge strides forward towards Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and achieving our mission, laying the foundation to lead in the AGI era. We continued to transform our Foundation, innovate, move forward on our AGI R&D roadmap, build the decentralized infrastructure for the global economy, embrace open collaboration, and guide the development of neural-symbolic AGI in a beneficial direction. We formed the Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) Alliance, the largest open-source, independent AI R&D initiative, free from corporate and governmental control.
